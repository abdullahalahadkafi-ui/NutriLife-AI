Who We Are

Welcome to NutriLife AI, a pioneering health-technology platform dedicated to revolutionizing personalized nutrition and digital wellness.

Based in Bangladesh, we integrate modern Data Science with deep clinical nutrition insights to provide instant, precise health evaluations, helping individuals lead a balanced and healthier life.

Our AI-Driven Mission

Our core mission is to democratize preventive health awareness. Quality nutritional guidance should be simple, automated, and accessible to everyone.

Using custom algorithms, our engine analyzes physical metrics and BMI to generate immediate, actionable fitness reports tailored to your weight goals and health conditions.

Scientific Standards

Every calculation on NutriLife AI is built upon strict metrics certified by the World Health Organization (WHO) and global health research.

We continuously optimize our algorithms to eliminate nutritional guesswork, delivering fast, secure, and medically grounded health intelligence.
