Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 | Transparency in Health Data Science

1. Introduction

Welcome to NutriLife AI. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to safeguarding your personal data and handling user information with complete security and transparency. Operating as an intelligent health-tech platform, NutriLife AI offers Body Mass Index (BMI) calculations, nutritional evaluations, and personalized dietary guidance through automated algorithms.

By accessing or using our platform at nutrilife-ai.onrender.com, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms outlined herein, please refrain from using our digital health services.

2. Information Collection & Usage

NutriLife AI collects only the minimal parameters necessary to generate accurate, real-time health evaluations. Collected information is categorized into physical health metrics and technical data:

Health Metrics: We process your physical data (such as height, weight, age, and biological gender). This data is used exclusively to compute your BMI, determine your metabolic category, and align results with World Health Organization (WHO) benchmarks. We do not permanently store your health metrics on our databases. Once your browser session ends, all physical inputs are purged from active memory.

"NutriLife AI strictly enforces a zero-retention policy on individual health parameters to guarantee complete data confidentiality."

3. Log Files & Analytics

NutriLife AI follows standard web practices regarding server log files. These logs record standard browser traffic when users navigate our platform. Collected log data may include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and total clicks. This data is strictly non-personally identifiable and is utilized solely to analyze website traffic, administer the platform, and optimize system performance.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense

NutriLife AI may partner with third-party advertising vendors such as Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve relevant advertisements to users based on their visits to NutriLife AI and other websites across the web.

Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the official Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Please note that NutriLife AI has no direct access to or control over third-party cookies.

5. GDPR & User Rights

We ensure all users are fully aware of their fundamental data protection rights under international privacy standards (including GDPR):

  • Right to Access: You reserve the right to request copies of any personal data processed by our system.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request corrections for any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion or removal of your personal data under specific conditions.

6. Children's Data Protection

Safeguarding young users online is paramount. NutriLife AI does not knowingly collect or solicit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details on our platform, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly erase all such records from our systems.
